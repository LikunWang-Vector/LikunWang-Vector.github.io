I am an **Algorithm Engineer** at [Shenzhen Suanhai Technology](https://www.suanhai.com/), working on TPU operator development and toolchain optimization. I recently completed an M.Eng. in Information, Production and Systems Engineering at [Waseda University](https://www.waseda.jp/inst/ips/en/), where I worked in the Optimization Lab with Prof. Shintaro Yamasaki.

My research combines finite element analysis, topology optimization, and deep learning to study how bio-inspired structures carry load. I built a framework for predicting loading forces on fish vertebrae using CT-based models, COMSOL simulations, and neural networks — work I presented at **WCSMO-16** in Kobe, Japan.

I enjoy working across the stack: from numerical kernels and toolchains for specialized hardware to computer vision systems and scientific computing. I am a recipient of the **Monbukagakusho Honors Scholarship (JASSO)** and an alumnus of the UESTC–Waseda G-Course program.

I am always happy to talk about AI systems, computer vision, or computational design — [get in touch](/#contact).
